## 🚀 Executive Summary

**Symbeon Mission Control** is the reference platform for **Computable Organizations**.

Rather than treating organizations as disconnected tasks, documents, or ticket queues, Mission Control models operational reality as a **Directed Operational Graph** composed of **Operational Objects**, relationships, verifiable evidence, executable policies and institutional knowledge.

Built upon the **Mission Control Specification (MCS)**, the platform transforms operational execution into permanent organizational intelligence through deterministic governance, cryptographic evidence lineage and policy-driven decision making.

**Evidence Precedes Truth.**

---

## ⚡ Problem vs. Solution

| Traditional Organizations | Computable Organizations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Information Decay:** Operational knowledge disappears across emails, documents and ticket systems. | **Operational Graph:** Every object, decision and dependency becomes part of a computable graph. |
| **Passive Governance:** Policies exist as documentation and are verified only after failures. | **Executable Governance:** Policies are evaluated continuously before operational changes occur. |
| **Fragmented Reality:** Projects, meetings, decisions and evidence remain disconnected. | **Institutional Memory:** Evidence and history become permanent organizational knowledge. |
