# MCS-0006

## Knowledge Specification

### Version 1.0 – Epistemic Evolution Cycle

---

# 1. Purpose & Scope
Knowledge in Mission Control is not manually authored as isolated text documents. It emerges organically from operational execution and historical patterns.

# 2. Epistemic Evolution Lifecycle

Knowledge progresses through an ascending hierarchy of abstraction and authority:

```
Observation → Fact → Pattern → Knowledge → Template → Standard → Policy → Law
```

### Lifecycle Stages
1. **Observation**: Raw operational telemetry or human note.
2. **Fact**: Verified event tied to evidence.
3. **Pattern**: Recurrent occurrence across multiple tasks/projects.
4. **Knowledge**: Structured insight derived from patterns.
5. **Template**: Reusable operational blueprint automating the knowledge.
6. **Standard**: Recommended organizational best practice.
7. **Policy**: Executable governance rule enforcing the standard (`PolicyEngine`).
8. **Law**: Immutable Architectural Law (`MCS-0001 Chapter 0`).

# 3. Knowledge Extraction & Reuse

- **Automatic Extraction**: Triggered when a `Task` or `Release` transitions to `Completed`.
- **Knowledge Reusability**: Knowledge objects link back to originating decisions via `LEARNS_FROM` and `DERIVES_FROM` relations.
- **Deprecation**: Knowledge that is superseded by newer operational facts transitions to `Deprecated` status without deletion.
