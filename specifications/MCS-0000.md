# MCS-0000

## Mission Control Manifesto

### Version 1.0 – Foundational Principles

---

# 1. The Core Purpose
Transform every operational action into institutional knowledge.

Organizations do not merely build software or deliver services; organizations evolve operational intelligence. Every action, decision, meeting, and release must leave a traceable computational footprint.

# 2. Fundamental Axioms

### Axiom 1: Evidence Precedes Truth
No operational state exists without verifiable evidence. Truth in Mission Control is not claimed; it is proven.

### Axiom 2: Reality Is Computable
Objects describe reality. Relations explain reality. Governance governs reality. History preserves reality. Knowledge improves reality.

### Axiom 3: Projects Are Living Operational Systems
Projects are not static lists of tasks. They are interconnected, living operational graphs that evolve continuously.

### Axiom 4: History Is Immutable
What occurred cannot be erased. Mistakes and corrections alike form the append-only institutional memory of the organization.

### Axiom 5: Agents Propose, Humans Govern
Artificial Intelligence agents observe context, compute predictions, and propose actions. Humans retain ultimate authority over governance and execution.
