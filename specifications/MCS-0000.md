# MCS-0000

## Mission Control Manifesto

### Version 1.0 – Foundational Principles

---

# Normative Language Notice
The key words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **REQUIRED**, **SHALL**, **SHALL NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, **RECOMMENDED**, **MAY**, and **OPTIONAL** in the MCS specification suite are to be interpreted as described in **IETF RFC 2119**.

---

# 1. The Core Purpose
Transform every operational action into institutional knowledge.

Organizations do not merely build software or deliver services; organizations evolve operational intelligence. Every action, decision, meeting, and release must leave a traceable computational footprint.

# 2. Fundamental Axioms

### Axiom 1: Evidence Precedes Truth
No operational state **MAY** be considered valid unless supported by verifiable evidence. Truth **MUST NOT** be claimed; it **MUST** be proven.

### Axiom 2: Reality Is Computable
Objects describe reality. Relations explain reality. Governance governs reality. History preserves reality. Knowledge improves reality. All organizational state **MUST** be computational.

### Axiom 3: Projects Are Living Operational Systems
Projects **MUST NOT** be treated as static lists of tasks. They **MUST** be modeled as interconnected, living operational graphs.

### Axiom 4: History Is Immutable
History **MUST** be append-only. Prior states **MUST NOT** be overwritten or deleted. Mistakes and corrections alike **MUST** form the immutable institutional memory.

### Axiom 5: Agents Propose, Humans Govern
AI Agents **MAY** observe context, compute predictions, and propose actions. AI Agents **MUST NOT** execute governance-critical approvals without human confirmation. Humans **MUST** retain ultimate governance authority.
