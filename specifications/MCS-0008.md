# MCS-0008

## Governance Specification

### Version 1.0 – Computable Metrics & Mathematical Model

---

# 1. Purpose & Scope
This document formalizes the mathematical formulas governing the `PolicyEngine`, `GovernanceScore`, `MaturityModel`, and `RiskEngine`.

# 2. Mathematical Formulations

### 2.1 Governance Score
The overall Governance Score $G$ is the weighted mean of all operational metrics:

$$G = \frac{1}{\sum w_i} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (m_i \cdot w_i)$$

Where:
- $m_1$: Policy Compliance Rate
- $m_2$: Evidence Coverage Ratio
- $m_3$: Decision Traceability Index
- $m_4$: Dependency Health Index
- $m_5$: Knowledge Capture Ratio
- $w_i$: Relative weight of metric $i$

### 2.2 Maturity Level Mapping
- **Level 1 (Ad Hoc)**: $0 \le G \le 40$
- **Level 2 (Managed)**: $41 \le G \le 60$
- **Level 3 (Defined)**: $61 \le G \le 75$
- **Level 4 (Measured)**: $76 \le G \le 90$
- **Level 5 (Optimized)**: $91 \le G \le 100$

### 2.3 Operational Risk Index
Operational Risk $R_o$ for any object $x$ is computed as:

$$R_o(x) = \text{Impact}(x) \cdot (1 - \text{EvidenceScore}(x)) \cdot \text{ViolationWeight}(x)$$
